# Medical review rubric

- [ ] Every factual claim traces to a source and its evidence tier is noted.
- [ ] No forbidden superlative ("safest," "best," "scarless," "painless,"
      "guaranteed," "fastest recovery," "no complications," "superior
      outcomes") without specific evidence and explicit sign-off.
- [ ] Robotic surgery described as surgeon-controlled, never autonomous;
      never framed as automatically superior to laparoscopic/open surgery.
- [ ] Content is specific to the actual procedure/condition — not generic
      boilerplate copied across unrelated specialties.
- [ ] Benefits, limitations, risks, and alternatives all present together
      (never benefits alone).
- [ ] Urgent/red-flag warning signs are present and clearly distinguished
      from routine recovery information.
- [ ] Author and reviewer named (or explicitly "pending"), published date
      and last-reviewed date present.
- [ ] References listed, from NHS/NICE/professional-body/peer-reviewed
      sources where applicable.
- [ ] No patient-specific diagnosis or treatment recommendation implied.
- [ ] Status field is `draft`, `requires-clinical-review`, or `retired` —
      never `approved`.
