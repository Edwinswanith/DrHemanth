# Premium design rubric

1. Does it immediately look like a respected UK consultant surgeon's site?
2. Is Prof. Hemant Sheth unmistakably the central identity?
3. Is the appointment action obvious without feeling aggressive?
4. Does the design feel custom rather than template-based?
5. Is robotic surgery presented credibly rather than futuristically?
6. Are robotic and laparoscopic surgery presented fairly (neither framed as
   obviously inferior)?
7. Does every section have a clear, distinct communication purpose?
8. Is the typography genuinely refined (not just "a serif and a sans")?
9. Is mobile quality equal to desktop quality?
10. Are all trust claims supported by something verifiable on the page?
11. Are there any fake or placeholder elements anywhere?
12. Is the page fast (Core Web Vitals targets met)?
13. Can a keyboard-only user complete an appointment request start to finish?
14. Does the page remain useful and legible with all animation disabled?
15. Would a cautious patient feel informed, not pressured?

Reject on sight: excessive gradients, excessive shadows, glassmorphism
without a functional purpose, unnecessary animation, weak mobile hierarchy,
repetitive identical card grids, fake trust indicators, decorative "generic
medical" imagery, low contrast text, inconsistent spacing, excessively
rounded components, oversized empty hero sections, SaaS-dashboard visual
language, or visual complexity that hides important information.
