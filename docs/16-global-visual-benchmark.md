## Deep Review Synthesis

### Primary Patterns To Apply

- Header: calm navigation with appointment as the clear task, not a promotional banner.
- Hero: surgeon name and role first; media supports identity rather than replacing it.
- Doctor identity: portrait, title and evidence must be visible, but only when verified.
- Appointment interaction: use a small, serious form panel or route-level form with clear “request, not booking” language.
- Treatment presentation: expose conditions and choices with patient-readable summaries before dense detail.
- Mobile behaviour: first viewport must preserve identity, value statement and appointment action before video or secondary modules.
- Typography: keep Source Serif 4 for authority and Manrope for operational clarity; avoid overlarge headings inside compact controls.
- Colour: navy, ivory and teal should carry the system; bronze stays as a rule or small editorial accent.
- Grid: reflow modules before they become cramped; avoid seven squeezed columns.
- Image treatment: use approved authentic imagery or image-optional layouts, never grey placeholders in production.
- Trust evidence: verified facts, content governance and clear limitations are stronger than counters.
- Section rhythm: alternate dense operational modules with quieter editorial bands.
- Footer: maintain legal, treatment and appointment routes without unverified regulatory claims.

### Anti-Patterns To Avoid

- Luxury wellness language, guaranteed transformation, and “world-leading” phrasing without evidence.
- Product-led robotics where the device brand becomes more important than the surgeon.
- Discount-led healthcare conversion.
- Dense hospital portals that bury the patient’s next action.
- Inline testimonials, ratings, awards, locations or publications that lack verification.
