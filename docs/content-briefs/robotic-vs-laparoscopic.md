# Content brief — Robotic vs laparoscopic (`/robotic-vs-laparoscopic`, Phase E)

Accessible comparison table (real `<table>` markup) plus editorial framing.
Compare: surgeon control, camera view, instrument movement/articulation,
surgeon ergonomics, incisions, procedure suitability/complexity, typical
recovery considerations, risks, availability, cost considerations, evidence/
limitations, factors affecting treatment choice.

Conclusion must state that the right approach depends on the patient,
condition, procedure, surgeon's judgement, available equipment, and
evidence — never frame laparoscopic surgery as outdated or inferior, never
claim robotic surgery is automatically superior. Include a consultation CTA
without turning the page into a sales pitch.

This page has no direct old-site equivalent (confirmed by the audit — the
old site never built this comparison) — it is genuinely new content, not a
migration.
