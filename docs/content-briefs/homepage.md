# Content brief — Homepage

Goal: a cautious patient understands, within seconds, who Prof. Sheth is,
whether he treats their issue, and how to request an appointment — without
feeling sold to.

Sections (see `02-information-architecture.md` for the full 15 and
`08-design-system.md` for composition flexibility):

- **Hero:** name, interim-canonical title (until Phase E approval), a
  short patient-centred statement (no superlatives), primary "Request an
  Appointment" CTA, secondary "Explore Robotic Surgery" CTA, working
  appointment card, explicit "request not confirmed booking" + emergency
  warning.
- **Trust strip:** only verified-or-explicitly-pending items — qualifications,
  GMC (once verified), NHS role, professional memberships. No counters.
- **Surgeon introduction:** short, human, links to `/about`.
- **Robotic surgery introduction:** surgeon-controlled framing, links to
  `/robotic-surgery`. Do not overstate Prof. Sheth's personal role until
  `04-content-verification.md` clears it.
- **Robotic vs laparoscopic comparison (teaser):** balanced, links to the
  full comparison page.
- **Treatments/conditions:** links into the consolidated treatment pages.
- **Why this practice / patient journey / locations / patient stories /
  research / FAQs / final CTA:** per IA — patient stories blocked until
  consent confirmed, use a clearly labelled pending state rather than
  fabricated quotes in the meantime.

Content status: this page ships in Phase C with structurally complete
