# 17 - Homepage Composition Plan

This plan preserves the required 15 semantic modules while avoiding 15 equal rectangular sections.

## Composition 1 - Header

Modules: Header. Restrained ivory bar, five primary groups, visible appointment action, accessible mobile menu and separate mobile appointment route.

## Composition 2 - First View

Modules: Hero. Desktop uses a 5/3/4 grid: identity and credentials, approved portrait slot, deep-ink appointment form. Mobile order: identity, title, copy, appointment drawer CTA, robotic link, portrait slot, trust evidence.

## Composition 3 - Evidence And Profile

Modules: Trust evidence, surgeon introduction. A narrow evidence ledger leads into an editorial two-column profile with a non-fabricated image requirement and cautious biography copy.

## Composition 4 - Robotic Education

Modules: Robotic surgery introduction. Midnight-ink section with surgeon-control explanation, image requirement, control sequence and benefits/limitations/suitability summary.

## Composition 5 - Surgical Choice

Modules: Robotic versus laparoscopic comparison. Neutral structured table on desktop and stacked factors on mobile. No winner state, no check/cross pattern.

## Composition 6 - Treatment Directory

Modules: Treatments and conditions. Numbered desktop selector with active detail panel; mobile stacked disclosures with summaries visible and direct treatment links.

## Composition 7 - Care Model

Modules: Why patients choose the practice, patient journey, practice locations. Evidence-led rows, stepped journey and selector-led location detail.

## Composition 8 - Proof And Education

Modules: Patient stories, research/publications/education, FAQ. Consent-held testimonial slot, pending academic index and two-column FAQ support pattern.

## Composition 9 - Conversion And Governance

Modules: Final appointment CTA, footer. Restrained deep-ink close, appointment and telephone options, legal routes, treatment navigation and pending professional/regulatory notes.
